## 2. What changed — behaviour before and after

- **Before:** Multiple suite assertions verified constants (`batchSize = 51; expect(batchSize).toBeGreaterThan(50)`), empty branches (`if (0) {}`), tautological failure guards (`if (!existsSync(...)) { expect(true).toBe(false); }`), overlap-only `.includes()` recall checks in `tests/detection-benchmark.test.ts`, and inline pre-fix bug simulations in `tests/adversarial-m2-rescan.test.ts` that asserted the historical buggy outcome on local arrays instead of calling `src/content/content.ts` or `src/content/normalization/mutation.ts`. Notably, `B5.5` claimed to verify 52 `characterData` text mutations without ever creating a `Text` node or calling `content.ts`, allowing `P1` to survive unnoticed.
- **After:** All `127` `tests/*.test.ts` files were audited. Every identified vacuous, inline-stub, or overlap-only assertion across 7 test files now executes real production modules (`src/content/content.ts`, `src/content/normalization/mutation.ts`, `src/content/pasteGuard.ts`, `src/content/detect.ts`) and fails when the underlying fixes (`P1`, `P2`, `P5`) are reverted. `tests/detection-benchmark.test.ts` also includes `josé.maría@example.test` and enforces strict exact-span equality (`m.start === expectedStart && m.end === expectedEnd`). `CONTRIBUTING.md` codifies the **Testing Standards & Anti-Vacuity Policy** going forward.
