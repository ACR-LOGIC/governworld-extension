One deliberate surface: find what's sensitive, decide what to mask, and keep an auditable record of the choice.

Page scanning Scan the current page's visible text with one click and review every finding with confidence, category, and masked preview.
Document redaction Drop in a PDF, image, or Word file and download a flattened, blacked-out copy. The original is never modified.
Preset policies HIPAA, PCI DSS, GDPR, Developer, or fully custom category selection — reverting to Custom the moment you adjust a box.

Redaction labels Optional white labels on mask blocks — [SSN], [DOB], [API KEY] — so you always know what was covered.
Local Logic Wizard & Community Author custom regex patterns locally using sample text. Raw examples stay on your device. Community sharing is disabled until server authorization is configured.

Security posture

Fail-closed, least-privilege, and auditable — the extension never guesses about your data.

By design

  • Minimal permissions

    activeTab, scripting, storage, downloads, offscreen, sidePanel — and zero host permissions. It never reads every tab.

  • No egress for scanning

    Scanning and OCR run on-device. The only optional network surface is opt-in account linking.

  • Pages are never mutated

    Masks are in-browser overlays. Redacted documents are flattened copies.

  • Fail-closed everywhere

    Unknown messages, malformed settings, and unrecognized keys are rejected — never silently allowed.

About

Who builds this, and why it's local-first.

ACR LOGIC

GovernWorld is built by ACR LOGIC (founded by Andres Chavez Ramirez). We build security and privacy tooling that keeps sensitive data governed at the point of use — on the device, under your control — while staying auditable and policy-driven for teams that need compliance.

View the project on GitHub

Why local-first?

The fastest way to protect data is to never expose it. On-device detection avoids the two biggest governance risks: accidental uploads and third-party persistence. Managed plans extend the same governance to AI traffic when you need server-side controls.

Verification

Detection is pattern-based and runs locally. It is not guaranteed to find every sensitive value. Always compare the redacted output against the original before sharing or submitting.

The audit log is metadata-only and signed with ECDSA P-256, so any post-hoc edit breaks verification.
