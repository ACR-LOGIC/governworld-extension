07

Your data stays on your device

All detection and redaction happens locally, inside your browser. GovernWorld ships with no host permissions and no remote endpoint for content. Your page text, documents, and images are processed on your own machine and are not transmitted to us or to any third party. No AI model, and no third-party service, receives your content in order for the Extension to work.

This is a deliberate design choice, and it is the reason the Extension is safe to point at a patient record, a client contract, or a credential. Other products in this category work by uploading what you scan to their own servers so that a hosted model can read it; that means your sensitive data is in transit and on someone else's infrastructure, where it can be logged, retained, subpoenaed, or breached. GovernWorld does not do that, which is also why it can work with no network connection and no account.

The consequences are worth being explicit about: local detection cannot be silently improved by a server-side model update, some capabilities require manual review that a hosted service would automate, and the Extension is only as good as the patterns and local models it ships with. We accept that trade because the alternative puts your most sensitive material on a network it never needed to touch.

Optional account, cloud-analysis, and community-features exist for your convenience. Cloud analysis is disabled and locked off by default. Reading the Privacy Policy is the best way to establish exactly what leaves your device, if anything.
